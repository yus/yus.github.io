Method Types — Same Sum, Different Path

A method is not the product. The sum is invariant; the order carries the difference.
serial / monotonic syncopated permuted staggered paratonic (coined)

trajectory through the tensor: serial ────────────────► (straight, monotonic) syncopated ──┐ ┌──┐ ┌──► (jumps, but arrives) permuted ─┐ ┌─┘ ┌─┐ ┌──► (rearranged, no beat) staggered ──┐ ┌───┐ ─► (delayed, same end)
The head thought: the sum is not a feature of the method. It's a feature of the factors. The method is the path — monotonic or not. Two methods can share a product and share nothing else. The mirror reads the method, not the sum.
